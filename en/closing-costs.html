The True Value of Expertise

We believe in transparent pricing, therefore below you will find a brief explanation of the pricing of some of the concepts included in our estimates.

Trustee Fees

Our trustee fees are determined by referencing the average rates of the most widely utilized trustees in Baja California Sur. These fees cover the administration and safekeeping of your property trust (fideicomiso), which is required for foreign nationals purchasing real estate in Mexico's restricted zone.

Certificates of No Liens

To avoid surprises regarding the registration status of a property that might delay the closing process and generate additional costs, we request two (2) Certificates of No Liens per closing — one at the beginning of the process and the second to use at closing.

Notarial Fees

Notarial fees are determined according to the tariff established by the College of Notaries of Baja California Sur. These fees are standardized across the state and cover the legal formalization of your transaction before a licensed Notary Public.

Exchange Rate Considerations

For quotations, we consider recent exchange rate fluctuations. We try to keep costs as low as possible, while adding a margin that allows adjustments in case the exchange rate decreases in the future.

Important Note: Parking Spaces & Garages

To provide you with the most accurate closing cost estimate, please let us know if your unit and garage or parking space have different cadastral keys (property tax identification numbers). When this is the case, costs increase because an additional permit from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (SRE) must be requested. Furthermore, trustee fees also rise since the trustee bank considers the garage or parking space and the unit as two separate properties.
